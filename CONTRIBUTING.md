# Contributing

Draft v0.3.3 is seeking critical review, not endorsement.

Useful contributions include:

- human-rights, equality, accessibility, and affected-stakeholder review;
- AI safety, tool authorization, agent security, and incident response;
- legal and organizational accountability across jurisdictions;
- usability for nontechnical, low-literacy, multilingual, and disabled users;
- measurement validity, reproducible testing, and documentation provenance;
- coercion, workplace, child-rights, and high-impact-use analysis; and
- evidence that a proposed safeguard fails in practice.

For every change, state:

1. the problem and concrete failure mode;
2. who benefits and who may be burdened or excluded;
3. whether it changes the non-waivable Core, user charter, model record, or host control;
4. misuse and backward-compatibility implications;
5. evidence or test cases; and
6. unresolved disagreement.

Do not collapse framework safeguards, user values, AI suggestions, organizational rules, and technical permissions into one layer. Do not add a certification or aggregate safety score. Provider-specific requirements belong in environment profiles or adapters.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome when disclosed. A model-generated review is not independent assurance. Do not include private data, credentials, hidden system instructions, or private chain-of-thought.
