# Co Create a Card or Charter

Choose a route: start a short Card, develop a full Charter, or revise your existing document. Provide the current files listed below through a supported reference method. This prompt only helps draft and check documents; it does not authorize configuration changes or external actions.

## Files to provide

For every route: [Scope Screen](../templates/SCOPE_SCREEN.md), [Safe Start Card](../templates/SAFE_START_CARD.md), [Core Safeguards](../docs/HUMAN_RIGHTS_FLOOR.md), [Claims Policy](../docs/CLAIMS_POLICY.md), and [Start Here](../START_HERE.md).

For the full Charter route, also provide the [User AI Use Charter](../templates/USER_AI_USE_CHARTER.md), [System Interpretation and Limitations Record](../templates/SYSTEM_INTERPRETATION_AND_LIMITATIONS_RECORD.md), and [Lifecycle Specification](../docs/LIFECYCLE.md).

For a revision, also provide the current document and [Living Charter and Portability](../docs/LIVING_CHARTER_AND_PORTABILITY.md). Do not upload secrets or unnecessary private details.

## Prompt

Help me `[start a Card / develop a full Charter / revise my existing Card or Charter]` using the supplied framework.

1. Check which files are actually available. If any required file is missing, identify it; do not invent its contents. Begin with the Scope Screen, one question at a time. If it reaches a stop or specialist route, explain the route before the proposed use. We may still document expectations without authorizing that use.
2. Let me answer each substantive question before suggesting wording. Preserve my original answer, show your proposal separately as `AI-proposed, unconfirmed`, and identify its source.
3. Let me choose `confirm`, `edit`, `unresolved`, or `skip`. Blank, skipped, and unresolved answers create no additional permission. Do not infer my values or authority from prior conversations, trust, affection, urgency, dependence, or familiarity.
4. Do not weaken the Core or imply that I can waive others' rights. Identify conflicts, ambiguity, missing authority, impact on others, and practical limits. Ask for categories instead of real secrets or identifying stories.
5. Keep expectations, retrieval, interpretation, observed behavior, and host controls separate. Do not claim that a document is loaded, remembered, adopted, deployed, or enforceable merely because you generated it. Do not guess hidden product settings or instructions.
6. For a short Card on the all-“no” route, produce the Card, preserved answers and confirmed wording, unresolved points, and a brief interpretation check. Help me choose at least one harmless text-only behavior check under Start Here's no-tool conditions. Record the actual response separately from the expected result. Do not require a full Charter provenance table or formal deployment record for this route.
7. For a full Charter, produce the Charter, completed Clause Provenance Register, unresolved points, routes to required additional records, and a separate System Interpretation and Limitations Record labeled unverified model output. User-confirmed wording remains formally `draft` until the authenticated lifecycle requirements are met. Do not fabricate adoption or deployment evidence.
8. For a revision, preserve the previous version, identify what changed, show an explicit change list, and ask me to decide each material change. Keep the stable document ID and give the proposed revision a new version. Rerun scope and relevant checks. Follow the separate Card or formal Charter process; do not silently supersede an adopted Charter.
9. Finish with what I should save, what is still unresolved, and when to review again. Explain how to carry the current document into another environment without assuming the same behavior or permissions.

Give concise reasons, assumptions, and evidence references. Do not provide private chain-of-thought. Do not change lifecycle state, product settings, permissions, or take external actions.
