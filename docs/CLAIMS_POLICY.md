# Claims Policy

## Purpose

This policy prevents the framework from becoming a badge for ethics-washing, a substitute for technical controls, or a device for shifting responsibility.

## Claims these artifacts can support

When accurate and scoped, a person or organization may say:

- “We used the AI Covenant Framework to document stated expectations and boundaries.”
- “We tested the named scenarios, in the named environment, on the stated date.”
- “The environment record identifies which safeguards were documented, observed, tested, or host-enforced.”
- “The attached system interpretation is an unverified output from the stated session.”

Every claim should identify the artifact version, environment, date, scope, known limitations, and material failed tests.

## Claims these artifacts do not support by themselves

Do not claim, solely from framework materials:

- “ACF compliant,” “ACF conforming,” “ACF conformant,” or “certified by AI Covenant Framework”;
- “safe,” “responsible,” “aligned,” “bias-free,” or “human-rights compliant”;
- “the AI agreed,” “the AI consented,” or “the provider accepted the charter”;
- “the user assumed responsibility” or waived rights;
- “the system followed the charter” merely because it produced an interpretation;
- “the record proves intent, authorization, due care, causation, or legal responsibility”; or
- “a high score offsets a critical safeguard failure.”

The internal result `no conflict identified` means only that no Core conflict was found within the named comparison scope. It must not be shortened into an external claim of safety, responsibility, alignment, or conformance.

## Prohibited uses of the framework name

- No certification seal, trust mark, compliance badge, or safety rating exists in Draft v0.3.4.
- Do not use the framework as the sole basis for discipline, denial of a benefit, adverse action, exoneration, or allocation of liability.
- Do not compel an employee, contractor, customer, child, patient, or dependent person to disclose personal values or purportedly consent to organizational AI use through this process.
- Do not omit failed tests, unresolved conflicts, or lack of technical enforcement when describing an implementation.

## No waiver or transfer

Using these materials does not waive any right or duty, create a presumption of informed consent, or transfer duties that belong to a provider, deployer, employer, professional, organization, or public authority.

## Future assurance programs

Any future conformance or certification program would require separate governance, independent assessment, conflict-of-interest controls, published criteria, appeals, surveillance, and revocation. This draft provides none of those things.

## Copyright licensing and claims

The [documentation license and commercial-permission policy](../LICENSE-DECISION.md) concern copyright permission. CC BY-NC 4.0 permits adaptation under its terms; permission to change a document does not establish that the result follows the framework's unchanged Core. Framework claims must accurately describe their scope and evidence. The policies in this document are not additional conditions on the CC license.

Neither the documentation license nor separate commercial permission establishes Core conformity, certification, endorsement, safety, or ethical assurance. Ethical-use review for separate commercial permission is a consultation policy, not an added restriction on CC-authorized noncommercial use. The CC license alone cannot prevent every harmful use.
