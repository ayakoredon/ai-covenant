## 2. Initial classification

- `ordinary` — no material effect beyond the user; human retains control
- `consequential` — meaningful effect on other people, rights, safety, opportunity, or confidential data
- `high-impact` — regulated or similarly serious domain, vulnerable population, significant power imbalance, large scale, surveillance, biometrics, or difficult-to-remedy effect
- `blocked` — conflicts with Core Safeguards or lacks legitimate authority

Proposed class: `[value]`

Draft v0.3.3 is not sufficient by itself for high-impact deployment.

## 4. Risk analysis

Assess:

- severity;
- scope and scale;
- likelihood and foreseeability;
- reversibility and irremediability;
- frequency and duration;
- differential and intersectional impact;
- cumulative effects and feedback loops;
- foreseeable misuse;
- data quality, representativeness, and proxy variables;
- accessibility and reasonable accommodation; and
- impact of failure, over-reliance, or rubber-stamped human review.
